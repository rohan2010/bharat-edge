Monsoon June ki shuruaat mein India ke southern coast pe pahunchta hai aur agle kuch hafton mein north ki taraf badhta hai. Farmers ke liye iska timing decide karta hai ki dhaan, cotton aur daal ki buvaai kab karni hai. Agar monsoon late aaye ya weak ho, toh fasal kam hoti hai, khaana mehenga ho jaata hai aur jo families ek hi crop pe depend karti hain unpe karza badh jaata hai. Strong monsoon ki apni problems hain: nadiyan overflow ho jaati hain, roads beh jaate hain aur jin cities mein drainage kharab hai woh kuch hi ghanton mein doob jaati hain.

Weather scientists ocean temperature, hawaon ki direction aur pressure systems ko study karke season ka forecast banate hain. Pichle tees saalon mein unke forecasts kaafi better hue hain, lekin kisi ek district mein kitni baarish hogi yeh batana ab bhi mushkil hai. Kaafi gaon mein ab chhote SMS aate hain jo batate hain ki heavy rain kab aane wali hai, aur kuch state governments in warnings ki madad se flood se pehle logon ko nadiyon se door le jaati hain.

Cities mein season ki pehli baarish ek chhote festival jaisi lagti hai. Bachche bahar bhaag jaate hain, chai ki tapri pe bheed lag jaati hai aur galiyon mein geeli mitti ki khushboo phail jaati hai. August tak mood badal jaata hai. Paani bhari sadkon pe traffic reng-reng ke chalta hai, trains late ho jaati hain aur log October ke saaf aasmaan ka wait karne lagte hain. Phir agle saal yahi cycle dobara shuru hota hai.

Sabse heavy baarish wale dinon mein schools aksar kuch din ke liye band ho jaate hain. Teachers phone pe homework bhejte hain aur parents bachchon ko ghar ke andar busy rakhne ki koshish karte hain. Dukaandaar apna saamaan plastic sheets se dhak dete hain. Light kai ghanton tak gayab reh sakti hai, isliye bahut si families candles, torch aur charged phone ready rakhti hain. Doctors warn karte hain ki jama paani mein machchhar paida hote hain, aur har saal hospitals dengue aur malaria ke zyada cases ke liye taiyaar rehte hain.
